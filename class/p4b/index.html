    Where we started

    How might we

    How might we turn the music friends listen to alone into something they play together, so they find out what they share?

    Problem statement

    College friends each have a music identity, but listening stats get looked at alone and group music talk stays informal. They want a low-pressure way to find what they share and surprise each other.

    Top 3 · option A · play live

    Live party quiz

    Friends join a private room, connect Spotify, and answer questions only friends could get right.

    • "Whose #1 song this month is Espresso?"
    • Points for right answers, plus points when friends guess you
    • Ends on game cards: Most Loyal Listener, Top 0.5%
    • Best for: a dorm hang, a party, a road trip

    Top 3 · option B · come back daily

    Daily drop

    One question a day lands in the group chat. Everyone answers on their own time, and streaks keep the group playing.

    • Like Wordle, but the answer is one of your friends
    • No one has to be in the same room
    • Weekly standings in the chat
    • Best for: long-distance friends, busy weeks

    Top 3 · option C · see the group

    Taste map

    A living map of the friend group's music: who overlaps, who's the outlier, and a playlist of the songs everyone shares.

    • Compatibility score for every pair of friends
    • Updates each month like a group Wrapped
    • Made to screenshot and post
    • Best for: end of semester, new friend groups

    Feasibility: A already runs as a prototype. Spotify's Development Mode caps sign-ins at 5 accounts, so A added quick picks and data uploads as other ways in.

    Novelty: Wrapped is alone and Spotify Blend already makes shared playlists, which hurts C. Live trivia exists, but not built from your friends' own data.

    Impact: A starts a conversation in the room. B is lighter. C is something you look at, not do.

    Our selection

    SoundCheck, the live party quiz

    How it answers the HMW

    It takes each friend's own listening history and turns it into questions the group answers together, out loud, in one room.

    How it answers the PS

    Stats stop being something you look at alone. Every round reveals something about a friend, and the cards at the end give the group a story to argue about.

    What we kept from the others: B's season standings (a room that keeps working each week) and C's shareable results card.
